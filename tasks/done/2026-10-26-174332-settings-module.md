# Settings module

Move the voice settings out of `src/input.ts` into `src/settings.ts`, so the
element keeps the field, the buttons and the listening flow, and the mode logic
can be tested without a browser. The behavior does not change.

- `src/settings.ts` holds the listen and submit modes, their defaults and
  storage keys, `allowedModes`, `choose` and `store`, and a class that builds
  the settings arrow and the panel, places the panel, shows the allowed switches
  and gives the current listen and submit modes. The element creates it, puts
  the arrow next to the microphone button and tells it when listening starts and
  ends.
- The panel styles move with it.
- Tests: unit tests with node:test for `allowedModes` and `choose`, including
  the order, unknown words, a stored mode no longer allowed and failing storage.
  The browser tests stay as they are and pass.
