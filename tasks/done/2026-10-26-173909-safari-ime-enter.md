# Safari IME Enter

Do not submit on the Enter that confirms input method text in Safari, per
`architecture.md` (Text input: Enter submits; Delivery: Safari).

- Safari sends the Enter that ends a composition with `isComposing` false and
  `keyCode` 229. Enter with `keyCode` 229 is treated as part of the composition,
  like `isComposing`, and does not submit.
- Tests with Playwright: a keydown of Enter with `keyCode` 229 does not submit.
