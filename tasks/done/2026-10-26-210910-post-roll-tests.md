# Post-roll tests

Test two cases of the 300 ms after a release that have no test yet, per
`architecture.md` (Voice input: Listening; Connection). Only tests change.

- A toggle press kept during the post-roll: a `hold` utterance ends, and a
  screen reader click, which is a toggle press, comes within 300 ms. After `end`
  is sent, a toggle session starts and stays on after the click.
- A connection close during the post-roll: the element shows "Voice failed", and
  a press made in that time still starts listening, on a new connection.
