# Early resume

Start the AudioContext while the press still counts as a user action, per
`architecture.md` (Delivery: Safari is supported).

- WebKit rejects `AudioContext.resume()` with `NotAllowedError` when it is
  called after awaits, because the press no longer counts as a user action.
  `startCapture` calls `resume()` right after creating the AudioContext, in the
  press, and awaits its result later, within the 5 second start limit.
- Tests: the three browser tests that fail in WebKit today pass in all three
  browsers: "opens a new connection on the next press after a close", "shows
  Voice failed after a close during an utterance" and "is ready again after
  being removed and added while listening".
