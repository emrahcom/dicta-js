# Voice shortcut

Add Ctrl+Shift+Space in the text field, per `architecture.md` (Voice input).

- In the text field, Ctrl+Shift+Space works like a press of the microphone
  button, and releasing Space like its release: held in `hold`, pressed in
  `toggle`. Key repeats are ignored. The key does not type a space. The focus
  stays in the field.
- It works only when voice input is on.
- The field has `aria-keyshortcuts="Control+Shift+Space"`, and the button has
  the tooltip "Speak (Ctrl+Shift+Space)".
- README: the shortcut.
- Tests with Playwright: hold and toggle with the shortcut, no space typed, the
  focus kept, no effect without voice input, and the two attributes.
