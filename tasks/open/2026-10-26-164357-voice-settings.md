# Voice settings

Move the listen mode select and the review checkbox into a voice settings panel,
per `architecture.md` (Element, Voice input: Switches).

- A small arrow button attached to the microphone button, with the accessible
  name "Voice settings" and `part="voice-settings"`. It opens and closes a
  popover panel, using the Popover API, so Esc and a click outside close it too.
  It is disabled while the element listens, and hidden when the panel would be
  empty.
- The panel holds a radio group named "Listen mode", with "Hold to talk" and
  "Hands-free" and `part="voice-listen-mode"`, in place of the select, and the
  "Review before sending" checkbox. Each is shown under the same rules as today.
  A choice does not close the panel. Storage stays the same.
- The microphone button and the arrow have no gap between them and share a
  border, so they look like one control.
- README: the panel and the new part.
- Tests with Playwright: the arrow opens and closes the panel, Esc closes it,
  the arrow is hidden with nothing to choose, disabled while listening, the
  radio choice is used and stored, the checkbox still works, and the parts.
  Existing tests that use the select or the checkbox open the panel first.
