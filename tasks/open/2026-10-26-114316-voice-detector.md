# Voice detector

Find utterances in a stream of audio frames, per `architecture.md` (Voice input,
`toggle`). Only the detection; the element uses it in the next task.

- Input: the 50 ms frames from `PcmConverter`.
- Speech is a frame whose level is clearly above the background noise level,
  which the detector learns from the quiet frames. The browser's noise
  suppression, already turned on, helps. No dependency and no model; a stronger
  method can replace it later behind the same interface.
- Output, per frame: nothing while it is quiet; at the start of speech, the
  frames of the last 300 ms and the current one; during speech, the frame; after
  800 ms of silence, the end of the utterance. Quiet frames are not passed on,
  except as the 300 ms before speech and the silence inside an utterance.
- Tests with Node's test runner on generated audio: silence and steady noise
  give no speech; a tone burst gives one utterance with the 300 ms before it; a
  pause shorter than 800 ms keeps one utterance; a longer pause makes two.
