# Audio converter

Convert microphone audio to the contract's format, per `architecture.md` (Voice
input). Only the conversion, without the AudioWorklet around it.

- Input: mono float samples (-1 to 1) at the microphone's rate, in chunks of any
  size, as an AudioWorklet delivers them.
- Output: 50 ms frames of 16 kHz signed 16-bit little-endian PCM (800 samples,
  1600 bytes), plus the shorter rest when the utterance ends.
- Downsampling averages the input samples that fall into each output sample, so
  it works for any input rate of 16 kHz or more (44.1 and 48 kHz in practice).
  Samples outside -1 to 1 are clipped.
- Tests with Node's test runner: frame sizes for 16, 44.1 and 48 kHz, chunk
  boundaries that do not match frames, the rest at the end, clipping, and a sine
  wave that keeps its frequency.
