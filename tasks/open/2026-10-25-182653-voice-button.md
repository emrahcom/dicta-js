# Voice button

Add the microphone button and wire it to the capture and the connection, per
`architecture.md` (Element, Voice input, Connection). The status region and the
failure display come in the next task.

- Button: shown when `inputs` includes `voice`, with an accessible name and
  `aria-pressed` while the user speaks. `voice-mode` `hold` (pointer or Space or
  Enter held) and `toggle` (one press starts, the next ends).
- Start: starts the capture and, when there is no open connection, opens one
  with `server`, `lang` and `getToken`. Frames go to the connection.
- End: 300 ms more capture, then the capture stops and `end` is sent. Presses
  during these 300 ms are ignored.
- Caption above the text field with the interim text. A final clears it and,
  when its text is not empty, sends `dicta-text` with `input: "voice"`.
- A connection close during an utterance stops the capture and clears the
  caption; the next press opens a new connection.
- Tests with Playwright and a fake dicta-server in the test: both modes with
  pointer and keyboard, frames and `end` reaching the server, interim text in
  the caption, the voice event for a final, no event for an empty final, and a
  new connection after a close.
