# Voice status

Show and announce the voice state, per `architecture.md` (Voice input,
Connection, Accessibility).

- A visible status line with `role="status"`, so screen readers announce its
  changes:
  - "Listening" from the start of an utterance until `end` is sent.
  - The text of each voice final, so the user sees and hears what was
    understood.
  - "Voice failed" after a close, a connection failure or a microphone failure
    during an utterance, until the next press.
- The text "Listening" is what shows the speaking state, not a color.
- A visible focus outline on the text field and the button.
- Tests with Playwright: each status text at its moment, the role, and the focus
  outline.
