# Send button

Add the Send button and keep the focus in the text field, per `architecture.md`
(Element, Text input, Voice input).

- A Send button after the microphone button, shown with the text field, with the
  accessible name "Send", an icon and `part="send"`. It submits the field like
  Enter, and does nothing while the interim text is shown.
- A pointer press on the microphone button or the Send button does not take the
  focus from the text field. The buttons still take the focus by keyboard.
- README: the Send button and its part.
- Tests with Playwright: Send submits like Enter, the button is hidden without
  text input, it does not submit during the interim text, a click on either
  button leaves the focus and the cursor in the field, and the part.
