# Idle close

Close idle connections, per `architecture.md` (Connection).

- When listening ends, a one-minute timer starts. When it fires, the element
  closes the connection; a new press first stops the timer. A closed connection
  is replaced on the next press, as today.
- The close does not show "Voice failed", since the user is not listening.
- Tests with Playwright, with a fake clock: the connection is closed one minute
  after listening ends, a press before that keeps the same connection, and the
  press after the close opens a new one without "Voice failed".
