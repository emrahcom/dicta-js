# Touch and panel

Fix touch use and the panel position, per `architecture.md` (Delivery: mobile
browsers; Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA).

- The settings arrow is at least 24 px wide, the WCAG 2.2 AA target size.
- The panel opens below the arrow when the arrow is in the upper half of the
  viewport, and above it otherwise, so it stays on screen when the element sits
  at the bottom.
- The microphone button has `touch-action: none`, `user-select: none` and
  `-webkit-touch-callout: none`, so a hold on a touch screen is not turned into
  a scroll, a selection or a callout.
- Tests with Playwright: the arrow width, the panel above the arrow near the
  bottom of the viewport, and the three properties on the button.
